About Me

I am a web developer who combines visual design choices with practical accessibility standards so people can move through a website without friction.

My process blends layout planning, semantic HTML, contrast validation, and usability testing to deliver pages that are both expressive and dependable.

Accessibility Practices

Keyboard flow, label association, fieldset grouping, and error feedback planning.
